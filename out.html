Up or down? It's a coin flip.

We tested popular trend rules on 55 years of gold prices and none could reliably tell whether the next month goes up or down, so we do not guess; gold rose in about 52 of every 100 months.

That is why the ranges above stretch both ways from today.

How often were we right?

We replayed this on 1,220 past days, each time using only prices known on that day. For the 1-month range, the price ended inside our "most likely" range 69 times out of 100 (we aim for about 68). It stayed inside the wider range 94 times out of 100 (we aim for about 95).

›How does this work?

Our method works by watching how jumpy the price has been lately, then checking itself against real past results. When gold has been calm the ranges get narrower, and when it has been jumpy they get wider.

We replay the same method on many past days and stretch or shrink the ranges until they came true about as often as we say.

These are ranges, not promises: surprise news can push gold outside them, and nothing here says which way it will go.
